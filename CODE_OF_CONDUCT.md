# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient; assume good faith.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not of people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone
represents the project. Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
hello@playcyclorama.com. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may ban anyone from the project, temporarily or permanently, as the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
